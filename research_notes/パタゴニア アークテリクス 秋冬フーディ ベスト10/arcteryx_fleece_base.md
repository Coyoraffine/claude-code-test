## Q1. Which Arc'teryx fleece/baselayer hoodies exist in the current (FW2026) lineup, and which have been discontinued/renamed since 2023?

### Takeaway
The current core lineup is the **Kyanite family** (Kyanite Hoody, Kyanite Pullover Hoody, Kyanite Lightweight Hoody [men's; women's version is called Kyanite LT Hoody]), the **Delta family** (Delta Hoody, Delta Pullover Hoody, Delta Hybrid Hoody, Delta Jacket; now built on Octa grid fleece), the **Rho baselayer family** (Rho LT Hoody men's / Rho Hoody women's, updated for 26/27; Rho Heavyweight exists as a Zip Neck, not a hoody), plus lifestyle fleeces **Covert** and **Cormac Heavyweight Hoody**. The older **Delta LT Hoody** has been superseded by the Octa-based Delta Hoody; no evidence was found that "Kyanite AR", "Rho Heavyweight Hoody", "Konseal Hoody", "Satoro Merino Hoody" or "Ionia Merino Hoody" are in the current lineup.

## Q3. Which are closest in everyday comfort/feel to the Patagonia Capilene Cool Daily Hoody but warmer?

### Takeaway
For a "soft, smooth, light, wear-all-day" feel like the Capilene Cool Daily Hoody, the closest Arc'teryx options are the **Kyanite Lightweight Hoody** (light Torrent stretch fleece, ~295 g, ¥25,300) and the **Rho LT Hoody / Rho Hoody** (brushed Torrent baselayer, next-to-skin). ## Q4. Best as a single outer layer for 5–15°C town use vs. as a mid layer under a shell in colder/active use?

### Takeaway
**Town single layer, 5–15°C:** Kyanite Hoody (dense, nylon-faced Power Stretch Pro, regular fit, warmest) for the colder end; Kyanite Lightweight Hoody for 10–15°C or active commuting. Add the **Delta Hybrid Hoody** if wind protection matters. **Mid layer under a shell / high output:** Delta Hoody or Delta Pullover (grid, 210–260 g, most breathable), and Rho LT / Rho Hoody as a warm base layer on cold, lower-output days.

### Cited Findings

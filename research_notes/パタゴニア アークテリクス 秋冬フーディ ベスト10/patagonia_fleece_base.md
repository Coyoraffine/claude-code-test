## Q1. Which Patagonia fleece and baselayer hoodies are in the 2026 lineup, and which have been discontinued or renamed since 2023?

### Takeaway
In late 2026, the technical-fleece hoodies reported as current in Japan are the **R1 Air Full-Zip Hoody, the R1 Thermal Full-Zip Hoody, the R1 TechFace Hoody and the R2 TechFace Hoody** (the women's line has the **R1 CrossStrata Hoody** instead of a TechFace). The baselayer hoodies are the **Capilene Air Hoody** and the **Capilene Thermal Weight Hoody**. The plain **R1 Pullover Hoody** was redesigned for SS2025, but a 2026 article says it, the R1 Full-Zip Hoody and the plain R2 Jacket **could not be found on patagonia.jp**, so they may have ended (this conflicts with the SS2025 launch and is unconfirmed).

## Q3. Which feel most like the Capilene Cool Daily Hoody (soft, everyday-wearable as a single layer)?

## Q4. Which work best as a single outer layer for 5–15 °C town use, and which as a midlayer under a shell in colder or active use?

### Takeaway
For **5–15 °C town use as an outer layer**, the wind-resistant faced fleeces work better: the **R1 Thermal Full-Zip Hoody** (warmest, smooth face sheds wind and light snow), the **R1 TechFace Hoody**, or the **R2 TechFace Hoody** for colder days. The air-permeable **R1 Air** and the **Capilene** hoodies feel drafty in wind on their own. **As a midlayer under a shell** for active winter use, the **R1 Air** (most breathable) and the **R1 Pullover Hoody / Capilene Thermal Weight Hoody** (grid fleece, light) are best.

### Cited Findings
- R1 Air: "lacks weather resistance", "little to no wind resistance". Reviewers see it as a breathable midlayer for active cool-weather use, not a standalone or windproof layer — [Switchback](https://www.switchbacktravel.com/reviews/patagonia-r1-air-full-zip-hoody); [SectionHiker](https://sectionhiker.com/patagonia-r1-air-full-zip-hoodie-review/)
- R1 Thermal: works as a standalone outer layer in cool conditions and as a midlayer — [GearJunkie](https://gearjunkie.com/apparel/patagonia-r1-thermal-hoody-review)
- R1 TechFace: weather and abrasion resistance added to R1 warmth — [Naturum](https://naturum.co.jp/product/?group=1&itemcd=3458727)
- R2 TechFace: warmth limits its appeal to truly cold days — [Switchback](https://www.switchbacktravel.com/reviews/patagonia-r1-techface-hoody). R1 for sweaty activity; R2 for cold days or low activity — [n-tarako](https://n-tarako.com/patagonia-r1-vs-r2/)

### Inferences
- **Tokyo or Osaka in Nov–Feb (daytime highs about 10–17 °C, lows 2–8 °C):** for commuting and town use as a single outer layer, the **R1 Thermal Full-Zip Hoody** or the **R1 TechFace Hoody** balance wind resistance with some breathability. The R1 Air alone may feel cold in wind below about 10 °C, but it is good for 10–15 °C or for brisk walking.
- **Colder or active use (hiking, snow, cycling):** use the **R1 Air** or the **R1 Pullover Hoody** under a wind or rain shell; add a **Capilene Thermal Weight or Capilene Air Hoody** as the next-to-skin layer.

## Q5. What do reviewers and users say about breathability (clamminess) and warmth-to-weight?

### Takeaway
